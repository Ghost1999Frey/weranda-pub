<idea>
  **Design Movement**: **Brutalist Punk**
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Raw Authenticity**: Unpolished, direct, and honest presentation.
  2. **High Contrast**: Stark differences between elements to create tension.
  3. **Structural Disruption**: Breaking the grid intentionally to reflect the punk ethos.
  4. **Textural Depth**: Using noise, grain, and paper textures to simulate a zine aesthetic.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Primary**: Off-white (#F5F5F5) for that "cheap paper" feel.
  - **Secondary**: Deep Black (#0A0A0A) for high-impact text and borders.
  - **Accent**: Electric Teal (#00A896) as seen in the reference, but used aggressively for highlights and backgrounds.
  - **Intent**: To evoke the feeling of a DIY punk flyer or zine found in a dive bar.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Asymmetric Balance**: Elements are not centered but balanced by visual weight.
  - **Overlapping Layers**: Images and text might slightly overlap to create depth and a "paste-up" look.
  - **Bold Borders**: Thick, defined borders around sections and images.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Torn Edges**: CSS clip-paths to simulate torn paper edges on sections.
  - **Halftone Patterns**: Background textures using SVG patterns.
  - **Sticker Art**: UI elements that look like stickers placed on the screen.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Hover Agitation**: Elements might jitter or skew slightly on hover.
  - **Hard Cuts**: Transitions are instant or very fast, no smooth fades.

  **Animation**:
  - **Glitch Effects**: Subtle text glitches on section headers.
  - **Marquee Scrolling**: Scrolling text for announcements or menu items.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: **'UnifrakturMaguntia'** or **'Pirata One'** (Blackletter style) for that gothic/punk vibe, or a distressed serif like **'Special Elite'**.
  - **Body**: **'Courier Prime'** or **'Space Mono'** for a typewriter/code aesthetic.
</idea>

  **Design Movement**: **Swiss Punk (International Typographic Style with a Twist)**
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Grid Adherence & Rebellion**: Establish a strong grid, then deliberately break it in key moments.
  2. **Typography as Image**: Large, bold type that acts as the primary visual element.
  3. **Negative Space**: Extensive use of white space to let the content breathe and create tension.
  4. **Functional Minimalism**: Every element has a purpose, stripped of decoration unless it serves the "punk" narrative.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Primary**: Stark White (#FFFFFF).
  - **Secondary**: Jet Black (#000000).
  - **Accent**: The specific Teal (#00A896) from the reference, used sparingly for buttons and links.
  - **Intent**: To create a clean, gallery-like space that lets the "punk" content (photos, menu items) stand out.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Modular Grid**: Strict column system.
  - **Vertical Rhythm**: Strong emphasis on vertical alignment and spacing.
  - **Split Screens**: 50/50 layouts for image/text pairings.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Huge Typography**: Section headers that span the entire viewport width.
  - **Line Separators**: Thin, precise lines separating content blocks.
  - **Monochrome Imagery**: Photos treated with high contrast black and white filters.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Smooth & Snap**: Smooth scrolling with snapping to sections.
  - **Inverted Hovers**: Buttons invert colors (black to white, white to black) instantly.

  **Animation**:
  - **Parallax**: Subtle parallax on images against the static grid.
  - **Reveal**: Text reveals from behind masks.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: **'Playfair Display'** (as in reference) but used in massive, bold weights.
  - **Body**: **'Helvetica Now'** or **'Inter'** (tightly tracked) for absolute clarity.
</idea>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: **Neo-Dada / Collage**
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Juxtaposition**: Mixing disparate elements (classical serif fonts with rough textures).
  2. **Anti-Design**: Questioning standard web conventions (e.g., navigation placement).
  3. **Playfulness**: The site should feel like a playground or an art project.
  4. **Organic Chaos**: Elements feel scattered but are actually carefully placed.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Primary**: Paper texture background.
  - **Secondary**: Ink Blue/Black.
  - **Accent**: Neon Green and Hot Pink splashes alongside the Teal.
  - **Intent**: To reflect the chaotic energy of a live punk show.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Freeform Canvas**: Elements placed "randomly" (using absolute positioning within relative containers).
  - **Z-Index Play**: Elements layering over each other dynamically.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Cutout Images**: Photos with backgrounds removed, looking like magazine cutouts.
  - **Hand-drawn UI**: Buttons and inputs that look hand-sketched.
  - **Tape Strips**: Visual elements that look like masking tape holding things up.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Draggable Elements**: Users can drag some stickers or photos around.
  - **Random Rotation**: Elements have slight random rotations.

  **Animation**:
  - **Stop Motion**: Animations that look like frame-by-frame stop motion.
  - **Wiggle**: Constant subtle motion on "loose" elements.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: **'Abril Fatface'** mixed with **'Permanent Marker'**.
  - **Body**: **'Anonymous Pro'**.
</idea>
